The heart and soul of the restaurant is a bustling open kitchen and the
striking marble-topped mirrored bar stocked with a connoisseur’s cabinet
of exclusive bottles. Twin stars of the kitchen are the rotisserie and grill;
fired up each morning, the grill adds flame and flair to the world-class
steaks, meat. Tucked away between the grandeur of Grosvenor Square and the stylish
boutiques of Mount Street, 34 Mayfair is a well-kept neighbourhood
secret just waiting to be discovered.
